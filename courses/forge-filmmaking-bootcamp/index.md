# Forge Filmmaking Bootcamp

## Overview

The Forge Filmmaking Bootcamp is a premium, immersive residential filmmaking program designed for aspiring storytellers, emerging filmmakers, career switchers, and creators who want hands-on experience in filmmaking.

This folder contains structured knowledge extracted from the official Forge Filmmaking Bootcamp training materials. It is intended for AI voice agents, admissions assistants, and support teams to answer applicant queries consistently and accurately.

## Agent Guidelines

When answering applicant questions:

- Answer only using verified information from the knowledge base.
- Never invent pricing, schedules, mentor names, or policies.
- Escalate whenever live or cohort-specific information is requested.
- Do not guarantee admissions, scholarships, placements, or career outcomes.
- Maintain a professional, supportive, and encouraging tone.
