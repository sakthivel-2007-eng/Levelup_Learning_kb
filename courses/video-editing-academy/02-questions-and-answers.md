# Questions and Answers

## Question 1

### What exactly is the Video Editing Academy?

It is a 12-week live online Video Editing Academy by LevelUp Learning designed to help learners build professional editing skills through live classes, mentor feedback, structured practice, and practical projects.

---

## Question 2

### Is this a live course or just recordings?

The academy is primarily a live cohort. Recordings are provided for revision, but the main value comes from live teaching, mentor interaction, accountability, and feedback.

---

## Question 3

### How long is the program?

The program runs for **12 weeks**.

---

## Question 4

### How many days a week are classes conducted?

The academy follows a **weekend format**, typically with sessions on **Saturday and Sunday**.

---

## Question 5

### What happens on Saturdays?

Saturdays are generally lighter sessions focused on:

- Mentor reviews
- Assignment feedback
- Doubt clarification
- Progress discussions

---

## Question 6

### What happens on Sundays?

Sundays are the primary learning sessions where the core editing concepts are taught in depth.

---

## Question 7

### What are the class timings?

The current schedule is generally:

- Saturday: **6 PM – 9 PM**
- Sunday: **3 PM – 7 PM**

Exact timings should always be confirmed with the admissions team.

---

## Question 8

### Is this online or offline?

It is a **live online** program.

---

## Question 9

### Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes.

The academy is designed for serious beginners as well as learners who already have some editing experience but want better structure and guidance.

---

## Question 10

### I don't know editing properly yet. Can I still join?

Yes.

The program is built for learners who want guided learning and consistent improvement. You do not need to already be an expert.

---

## Question 11

### Is this only for people who edit reels?

No.

The academy is broader than short-form content. It prepares learners for creator content, social media editing, long-form videos, and professional editing work.

---

## Question 12

The academy is highly practical.

Learners complete projects and assignments while receiving continuous mentor feedback.

---

## Question 13

Yes.

Mentor feedback is one of the core parts of the Video Editing Academy.

Yes.

## Question 20

### What makes this different from YouTube tutorials?

Unlike random tutorials, the Video Editing Academy provides:

- Structured learning
- Live mentor guidance
- Practical assignments
- Continuous feedback
- Accountability
- Portfolio development

These elements help learners improve consistently rather than learning in an unstructured way.
