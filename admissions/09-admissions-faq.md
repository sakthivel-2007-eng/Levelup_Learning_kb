# Admissions FAQ

## Is admission automatic?

No.

Programs may have a selection process.

---

## Does paying the application fee guarantee admission?

No.

It allows the admissions process to continue.

---

## Who decides selection?

The admissions team.

---

## Can I skip the interview?

Follow the process defined for the specific program.

---

## Can I speak to admissions?

Yes.

The AI can escalate the conversation when appropriate.
