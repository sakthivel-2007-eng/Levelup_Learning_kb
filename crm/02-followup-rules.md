# Follow-up Rules

## First Follow-up

Within 24 hours of the application.

---

## Second Follow-up

If there is no response, follow up after 48 hours.

---

## Third Follow-up

One final attempt after another 3–5 days.

---

## Agent Rules

- Be polite.
- Never pressure the learner.
- Reference previous conversations when appropriate.
- Stop follow-ups if the learner requests no further contact.
